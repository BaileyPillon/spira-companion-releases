# Spira Companion on AYN Thor

Install **SpiraCompanion.apk**. In **ARMSX2 → Settings**, search for **Enable PINE** and turn it on. Leave the port at **28011**. Start a supported PS2 disc in ARMSX2. In Cocoon, edit its game entry, choose **Spira Companion** under **Companion App**, and save with the check mark. The game opens on the top screen and the companion opens on the bottom screen.

The companion connects to ARMSX2's local listener at **127.0.0.1:28011**. Live tracking uses read commands. Once set up, it works on the handheld without USB or a PC. Optional Cheats, including AP sharing in 1.37, use separately guarded writes and are hidden by default.

**New tools in the 1.37 source build:** open **Workspace** to search, pin tools and keep playthrough notes. **Goal Plan** combines FFX goals and shared materials. Sphere Grid opens the editable sandbox; battle Status opens action comparisons. **Blitzball → Live match** reads the active match, and **Challenges** reads supported Butterfly/Catcher events. Profiles and JSON backups are under Workspace or Setup. See [coverage and pending gameplay acceptance](EXPANSION-1.37.md) before treating the new readers as device-verified.

**Live Coach:** start on **Status**. The first recommendation combines the current situation with recorded inventory and progress. Urgent recovery and pickup deadlines take priority. Expand **Live coach** to select **Story**, **Arena captures**, **Blitzball**, or **Completion** as your FFX focus. **Hide this tip** dismisses that situation for the session; a new battle can raise the same warning again. Other relevant opportunities remain under an expandable list. Fight details remain available below the turn order without repeating the leading advice.

**Follow Game automatically** is optional and off by default. Enable it under **Live coach** on Status or Setup. When the feed confirms a battle transition, Spira opens Status; an FFX Blitzball menu opens Team Builder and the pool opens Live match. Returning to the field restores the original tab and scroll position, including the retained guide iframe and its full-screen state. A manually selected tab or Blitzball subview cancels the automatic return. **Return to…** goes back early; **Stay here** keeps the current tool. Recent interaction, focused form controls, an explicitly paused guide, Setup and Cheats take priority. Pauses, feed gaps, disc changes and detectable save rewinds cancel automatic returns. Follow Game does not play the game or change saves. See the [behavior and validation notes](LIVE-COACH.md).

For **FFX International**, open **Status** in battle to see the current actor and the game's CTB forecast. Swipe the turn order sideways to see all 16 positions. The list follows the forecast as you browse commands. Aiming at an ally shows their HP and MP. Aiming at a fiend shows its HP, affinities, items you can steal and the damage threshold for an Overkill. A fight tip appears where available. Party lists learned commands. Items lists inventory quantities. Equip shows the equipped weapon and armor, plus alternatives you can filter by purpose. Bestiary filters by name or encounter area and marks fiends seen or defeated. Arena shows capture counts by region.

In either game, **Battle tactics · Recovery** appears on Status when the live party needs a heal, revival or supported status cure. Open it to see who needs the item and how many are in your bag. In FFX, **Equip → Compare gear** shows which owned pieces fit a goal such as capture, survival, or AP farming, and which abilities you would gain or give up.

Open **Boss Prep** from the sidebar or **Equip** to choose Evrae, Dark Aeons, Penance, or Don Tonberry AP farming. The view starts with your active party. **Plan for** lets you change the lineup for planning. Check which owned pieces fit, the combined material bill, and the Bestiary links for any shortages. The Don Tonberry plan reads your party's kill counts and Overdrive modes, and shows live capture requirements for Don Tonberry and One-Eye. The Penance section tracks Dark Aeon encounter clears and shows the arms' HP and forecast order when you fight Penance.

Choose **Boss Prep → Yojimbo · Zanmato** to see the save's compatibility, your gil, and the current foe's resistance bracket. Pick a boss to plan before a fight, or choose a resistance level. Enter a payment and set **Full Overdrive** to match the game. Spira shows the cheapest equivalent offer, the next affordable increase, and affordable targets for 50% and 90% odds. The estimate applies at the payment prompt if you chose “To defeat the most powerful of enemies” when recruiting him. Compatibility and Overdrive overrides are planner inputs. The sources are [Fahrenheit's PS2 save layout](https://github.com/fahrenheit-crew/fahrenheit/blob/2b291d0/src/core/ffx/savedata.cs), [Grayfox96's paid-turn model](https://github.com/Grayfox96/FFX-RNG-tracker/blob/main/ffx_rng_tracker/events/yojimbo_turn.py), and [the extracted Penance resistance data](https://github.com/EdgeSync/Yojimbo-Zanmato-Calculator/pull/1).

Sphere Grid reads each character's current position, activated nodes and available Sphere Levels. Use **Find character** to center the board, then tap a node or pick it from the list. The planner estimates the cheapest unlocked route using previously traveled links. The game shows the final move cost.

Use **Node filter** to find remaining unactivated, empty, or locked nodes on that character's Sphere Grid. Choose a node to center it, plan a route, and see the required sphere and your current stock. Open **Spheres for remaining nodes** below the grid for the full list of activation materials.

Open **Plan stat targets** under the Sphere Grid, select a stat, and enter a target up to 255. The estimate applies existing unactivated nodes first, then calculates how many new +4 nodes you need. It compares that with empty slots and inventory. **Plan farm** opens Arena with the required quantity filled in.

Open **Blitzball → Roster & contracts** for the Aurochs, opposing teams, free agents, and contracts ending within three games. **Scout** pairs the shortlist's locations with current contracts. **Rewards** shows Wakka's Reels, Sigil, and matching entries in the saved first-place prize lists. In **Arena**, tap a creation on any related capture row to see what it still needs and where to hunt those fiends.

Open **Arena → Hunt** or tap **Scout fiends** from Status in a supported area. Hunt follows your room and ranks nearby encounter tables by the captures you still need. Choose a species to compare spots across Spira. Expand a spot for its formation odds and pin the fiend to get a contextual suggestion in the full-screen Guide. Capture Assist shows equipped catchers and the targets you still need during fights. Tap **Start hunt journal** from the field to see observed encounters, confirmed captures and regional milestones until you close Spira or end the journal.

Open **Bestiary → Gear Drops** to compare fiends by the equipment they can drop. Choose a preset or open **Customize abilities and slots** to build your own. Switch **Finishing blow** to compare a character's final hit with an Aeon kill. The view estimates gear odds per kill and across repeat kills, checks matching pieces in your live inventory and links to customization materials. **Gear drops** on a selected battle target opens that fiend directly. The **Arena** Triple AP plan and **Boss Prep** Ribbon link open the relevant presets.

Tap **Watch** next to a Blitzball player to pin their live contract to **Watchlist**. The full-screen Guide can flag watched players with one game left in the Blitzball menu or a watched free agent who is nearby at a known recruiting location. The watchlist is stored on the device.

Open **Blitzball → Techniques**, choose an Auroch, and filter to **Opponent targets** to see which rival players have techniques equipped that your player has yet to learn. The list uses equipped slots across the saved opposing teams. Your matchup and the in-game Techcopy label determine which moves you can try to learn. The technique pages and equipped slots were cross-checked against [Farplane](https://github.com/topher-au/Farplane/blob/master/FFX/Data/Blitzball.cs).

Open **Blitzball → Team Builder** for a suggested starting six using your saved player levels. Start with **My roster**, or include free agents to plan upgrades. **All players · planning** also includes signed rivals: their contracts appear beside their names, and you must wait for their team to release them. A free agent may still be inaccessible at your story progress. The recruiting plan shows locations, learned techniques, watch buttons and how many roster spaces you need. It does not sign, release or reposition players in the game.

Expand **Keep favorite players in position** to reserve a place for Tidus or another favorite; the other five positions recalculate. Choices survive temporary pool changes for the current app session. **Compare** shows all eight stats side by side, signed differences and a position shortlist. **Preview Lv.** lets you compare future levels, including when no roster is loaded. Missing contracts and techniques remain unknown in reference-only previews.

Blitzball stats come from reference samples, not live match memory. Between-sample values are labeled **Estimated stats**. A preview below a recruit's starting level uses that starting level and labels it. Scores favor shooting/endurance for forwards, passing/speed for midfield, tackling/blocking for defenders, and catch for keepers. The exact weights are under **How the lineup is chosen**. Techniques are shown separately because they can change your preferred lineup. The [research and source notes](FEATURE-RESEARCH-1.35.md) explain the data and limitations.

In **Mix**, use **Quick mixes in stock** for common recipes such as Sunburst, Trio of 9999, and Super Mighty G. Open a result and pin an ingredient pair to keep it at the top with your current stock. Turn off **In stock** to browse unavailable pairs and tap **Find** beside a missing ingredient to see item sources. **Back to Mix** restores your search and stock filter. In **Collections → Completion Goals**, choose Trophy goals, Full-save goals, or Custom goals. Optional Aeons and Optional Bosses also have their own checklists.

Open **Challenges** from the sidebar, a nearby challenge on **Status**, or its weapon in **Collections**. Lightning Dodger shows the game's saved record. Catcher Chocobo shows the saved time, training completions and a balloon calculator. Butterfly Catcher highlights the current course and reads the sigil state from the save. Check off the Central and North chests as you open them after gaining free use of the airship. The checkboxes are remembered on this device across game saves. Village of the Cactuars follows the save's current clue, including Elio's move to the airship. The same view checks your No Encounters gear and finds material sources.

Open **Collections → Anima Treasures** to see which of the six temple chests the save records, where each statue stands at Baaj, and which Dark Aeons guard the returns. **Missables** and Anima's entry in **Optional Aeons** both link to the checklist.

Spira checks the Celestial Mirror, fully powered weapons, Anima, Arena creation requirements, AP weapons for the active party, and boss clears. Each step opens its existing checklist or farming plan.

Baaj links to Anima’s temple treasures and Omega links to Arena captures. ## Follow the offline FFX walkthrough

In Spira Companion, open **Guide → Choose walkthrough** and select **Download → Thor-Guides → FFX - Complete Offline Walkthrough.html**. This feature reads the existing October 16 offline Jegged export. The walkthrough itself is kept out of the APK and release ZIP.

With FFX International open in ARMSX2, the reader jumps when the game settles in a different mapped section. It gives you time to finish scrolling before moving and holds your place during ordinary battles. **Contents**, internal links, and **Pause follow** let you browse freely. Tap **Back to game** to return to your live position. Unknown rooms leave the current page in place.

Tap **Full screen** to read across the lower display with the app controls hidden. Tap **Exit full screen** in the lower-right corner to bring the controls back. The reader keeps its place as the text reflows.

In full screen, Spira can open Blitzball prizes from the menu, your roster and match tips from the pool, or the Scout page when a short-listed free agent is nearby. At Home’s airship dock it checks how many one-time Primers you still need. In battle it can point out a usable Mix, a Ronso Rage, or the last fiend you need for an Area Conquest. It can also show the current Cactuar clue, a nearby challenge, or map exits.

Spira gives you time to finish scrolling and spaces out routine hints. Pickup deadlines can appear while you read. Open a suggestion and tap **Back to full-screen guide** to resume the same passage. Suggestions fade and can be dismissed. Turn them off under **Setup → Suggest relevant tabs in full-screen Guide**.

On the S.S. Winno deck and around Besaid, the Guide can surface contextual reminders for Jecht Shot and Valefor’s Energy Blast. Check the game for those two unlocks.

## Optional Steam bridge

The earlier Steam HD Remaster bridge uses GameNative. It is a separate workflow from the PS2 discs. It recognizes the 2026 Steam executable builds. Full GameNative gameplay verification remains pending.

1. On the Thor, install **SpiraCompanion.apk** and copy **SpiraBridge.exe** to Android’s **Download** folder.
2. In GameNative, open the HD Remaster’s **Options → Open container**. In the Windows file manager, copy **SpiraBridge.exe** from **D:** to **A:** beside **FFX.exe**, **FFX-2.exe**, and **FFX&X-2_LAUNCHER.exe**. Exit the container.
3. Under **Edit Container → General → Executable Path**, note the existing selection and choose **SpiraBridge.exe**. Launch the Steam game entry, then pick **X** or **X-2** in its original launcher.
4. Open **Spira Companion** on the bottom screen. In **Setup → Choose folder**, select **Download → SpiraCompanion → Use this folder**. The bridge creates this folder on its first run. Select the subfolder itself so Android grants access to just that folder.
5. Load a save or start a new game. The header should change to **Live** and the Status view should show the front line as you play.

To use Cocoon, assign **Spira Companion** to the HD Remaster entry’s **Companion App** field. GameNative still controls the top-screen game launch.

## If it stays on Waiting

- Check for **D:\SpiraCompanion\live.json** inside the same container. Its Android equivalent should be **Download/SpiraCompanion/live.json**. If D: maps somewhere else, fix the drive mapping in that container or select the corresponding **SpiraCompanion** subfolder in Android.
- If the original launcher fails in your container, set **Executable Arguments** to **--x** or **--x2** to start the corresponding game directly. Clear that argument to return to the collection launcher.
- **At title screen** means the feed is connected. **Feed stopped** means the snapshot is stale. Relaunch through the bridge. **Unsupported build** means the game’s PE identity differs from the supported Steam profiles.
- To inspect the bottom-screen UI by itself, choose **Preview FFX** or **Preview FFX-2** in Setup. Preview data is labeled **Sample data**.

## Remove the Steam bridge

Restore the previous Executable Path and arguments in GameNative, remove **SpiraBridge.exe** from the game folder, uninstall the Android app, and remove the **SpiraCompanion** folder in Download if you no longer need it.
